# Festive Guide Context – India (Tamil Nadu)

This product is a local festive guide that explains how festivals
are celebrated in Tamil Nadu, India.

## Festivals Covered
- Diwali
- Pongal
- Navaratri

## Diwali
- Early morning oil bath
- Wearing new clothes
- Lighting diyas
- Bursting crackers in the morning
- Preparing sweets like laddu and mysore pak
- Performing Lakshmi & Ganesha pooja

## Pongal
- Harvest festival celebrated in January
- Prepare sweet pongal and ven pongal
- Decorate house with kolam
- Thank Sun God and cattle

## Navaratri
- 9-day festival for Goddess Durga
- Keep golu dolls
- Offer sundal daily
- Perform evening pooja

## Cultural Rules
- Festivals are family-oriented
- Food and rituals are important
